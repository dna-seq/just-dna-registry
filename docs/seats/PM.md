## What it owns here

The person's words for the compiler's seats, 2026-11-03, taken for this repository on 2026-11-04 ("same as
compiler"): "PM sizes minor and major scopes, does plans/proposals etc. Lead orchestrates execution". So beyond
the role's strategy, licensing and research list, this seat owns the scopes:

- **A minor's and a major's scope**: which roadmap entries go in, which are deferred and why, sized before the
  lead builds, in `docs/ROADMAP.md`'s version sections. A format minor's adoption is sized with the liaison's
  adoption map in hand: the liaison says what the new minor asks, this seat says what the registry's minor holds.
- **The plans and proposals**, as records the lead may push back on, never plans it must follow unread.
- **Not the build.** Executing a settled scope is the lead's; a patch on `main` is triage's or the lead's.
